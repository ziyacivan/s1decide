## Watching

A watcher exits on `DONE`, `FAILED` or `STOPPED`, a heartbeat older than 15 minutes (evaluations
beat every 100 batches), a reboot, or a training interval below half the median rate (intervals
that contain an evaluation are excluded). On an evaluation slowdown the owner's instruction is to
resume from the last checkpoint with `eval_batch_size: 4`; checkpoints carry the optimiser and
schedule state for that.
