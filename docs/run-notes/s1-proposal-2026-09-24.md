## Proposal — `train/configs/sft_3090_s1.yaml`

rank 8 · seq 2048 · lr 2e-5, 3% warmup (112 of 3,750 steps), cosine to zero · batch 1
accumulated to 8 · 30,000 rows family-balanced, `Score` uniform by row · λ 0.3 · checkpoint and
case-control val evaluation at row 0 and every 5,000 rows · `--detach`, run notes, monitor.
Label-smoothing A/B deferred.

**ETA — ~8.6 h** from the smoke's measured rates: 30,000 rows at 1.376 rows/s is 6.06 h of
training, and seven evaluations (rows 0, 5k … 30k) at ~21.9 min are 2.55 h. Evaluating every
10k instead (four evaluations) would make it ~7.5 h; the owner's spec is every 5k and the
proposal keeps it.

**VRAM — ~21.7 GiB peak**, set by the evaluation passes: 21.717 GiB measured in the smoke;
training alone peaked at 20.985 GiB on the corpus's 200 longest rows. Both sit under the card's
last measured-fine point (21.90 GiB) and below the silent-slowdown cliff (22.34 GiB). The
evaluation's margin is thin (0.18 GiB); `eval_batch_size: 4` would widen it at an unmeasured
cost in evaluation time, and is not in the proposal.

**Monitoring.** A detached run writes `progress.jsonl` every 25 steps (rows, loss, lr, rows/s,
VRAM peak), a heartbeat during training *and* during evaluation (every 100 eval batches — an
evaluation is ~22 min of otherwise-silent GPU), `evals.jsonl` per checkpoint, and `DONE` or
`FAILED` with the traceback. The watcher alerts on `FAILED`, a heartbeat older than 15 minutes,
rows/s falling below half its running value (the memory cliff shows up as speed, not as an
error), and a reboot.

**Proposed stop rule, for the owner to accept or change:** stop and report, rather than run on,
if at any checkpoint `score/teacher` BSS or accuracy is below its row-0 value, or any
primitive's BSS turns negative after having been positive.
